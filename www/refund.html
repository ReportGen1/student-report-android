REFUND POLICY

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains when a refund may be issued for a Basic, Standard, or Premium subscription purchased on the Student Report Sheet Generator. It should be read alongside our Terms of Service.

Eligible for a Refund

You may request a full refund within 48 hours of payment if:

  • You were charged in error or charged more than once for the same subscription.
  • You have not generated any student reports on the plan you purchased.
  • A technical fault on our side (for example, payment confirmed by Paystack but the subscription never activated on your account) prevented you from using the Service.

Not Eligible for a Refund

  • Requests made after 48 hours of payment.
  • Subscriptions where one or more student reports have already been generated, since that portion of the allowance has already been used.
  • Change of mind, or purchasing the wrong plan by mistake after reports have already been generated on it. In this case, contact us and we will do our best to help you switch plans instead.
  • Unused reports remaining at the end of a subscription period are not refundable, but they may be carried over automatically if you renew, as described in our Terms of Service.

How to Request a Refund

Email us at abdullahiibraheem075@gmail.com with the email address used on your account, the date of payment, and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

Approved refunds are issued back to the original payment method through Paystack and may take a few business days to reflect, depending on your bank.
