FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Student Report Sheet Generator

What is the Student Report Sheet Generator?

It's a tool that lets teachers and schools upload their students' scores in an Excel file and instantly generate formatted, printable report sheets, complete with your school's name, address, and logo.

How do I get started?

  • Create an account and choose a subscription plan.
  • Enter your school information and logo (optional, but it will appear on every report).
  • Download the Excel template.
  • Fill in your students' details and scores, then upload the completed file.
  • Select a student and generate their report, or generate all reports at once.

A subject sheet is created for each subject - why?

The Excel template gives each subject its own sheet, so you only need to list the students who actually offer that subject. A student who is not listed on a subject's sheet is treated as not offering it, and that subject will not be counted toward their average.

What subscription plans are available?

We offer Basic, Standard, and Premium plans, each with a different student/report limit and duration. Full details and pricing are shown on the plans page when you sign in.

What happens to unused reports when I renew?

Any reports you haven't used from your current plan are automatically carried over and added to your new plan's allowance when you renew.

Does regenerating the same report cost me another report?

No. If you generate a student's report again without changing their data, it's displayed again for free. You are only charged a report when it's genuinely new or the student's data has changed since it was last generated.

How do I pay, and is it safe?

Payments are processed securely through Paystack. We never see or store your card details.

What if I need a refund?

See our Refund Policy for eligibility and how to request one.

I forgot my password - what do I do?

On the sign-in page, click "Forgot Password?" and enter your account email. You'll receive a link to set a new password.

Is my students' data safe?

Yes. Your uploaded student data is used only to generate the reports you request and is never sold or shared outside the providers that run the Service. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I print or save a report as PDF?

Once a report is generated on screen, use your browser's Print option (or the keyboard shortcut Ctrl/Cmd + P) and choose "Save as PDF" if you want a PDF copy instead of a printed page. The report is formatted to print cleanly on A4 paper.

What file formats can I upload?

Only completed copies of our own downloadable Excel template (.xlsx or .xls) are supported, so the report generator can correctly read your students' scores.
