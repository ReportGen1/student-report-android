PRIVACY POLICY

Student Report Sheet Generator

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Student Report Sheet Generator ("the Service") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. The Service is operated by Ibrahim Abdullahi.

Information We Collect

Account information: your email address and password, used to sign in via our authentication provider (Supabase).

School information: your school's name, address, and logo, which you choose to enter so it can appear on generated report sheets.

Student data you upload: names, class, and scores contained in the Excel file you upload, used only to generate report sheets for the students you enter.

Subscription & usage records: your chosen plan, payment reference, and the number of reports you have generated, used to enforce your plan's allowance and provide support if you contact us.

Payment information: payments are handled directly by Paystack. We do not receive or store your card number or banking details - Paystack shares only a payment reference and status with us.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account, and let you sign back in.
  • To generate the report sheets you request, and to let you re-display a previously generated report without being charged again.
  • To track and enforce the report allowance included in your subscription plan.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To send account-related messages, such as password resets or subscription confirmations.

We do not sell your data, your school's data, or your students' data to anyone, and we do not use student data for advertising.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Users account and subscription details is stored in our database, hosted by Supabase. Some information (such as the record of which reports you have already generated) is also kept in your browser's local storage on the device you use, so the Service works smoothly between visits.

Sharing With Third Parties

We share only what is necessary to run the Service:

  • Supabase - hosts our database and handles account sign-in.
  • Paystack - processes subscription payments.

We do not share student data with any other third party, and we do not use it to train external services.

Your Rights

You may request a copy of the information we hold about your account, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and associated data, in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR). To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Data Retention

We keep your account and subscription records for as long as your account is active, so your report history and allowance stay accurate. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your account and uploaded student data, except where we are required to keep payment records for accounting or legal purposes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
