What the generator handles for you

Class list

Enter your students once for each class and session. Names carry over to every subject sheet and across all three terms, with Admission No as an optional second key.

Score entry

Each subject teacher fills in their own subject sheet, where the class students are already listed. Enter 1st CA, 2nd CA, and exam scores the way you already record them.

Name matching

Extra spaces and formatting slips in names are cleaned up automatically, and two students with the same name are told apart by their Admission No.

Grading

Subject totals, subject averages, overall averages, and class positions are calculated automatically. Positions are ranked by average, and a subject a student doesn't offer is left out of their average, not counted against them.

Comments

Class Teacher's and Principal's comments are filled in automatically from each student's average when you upload the scores.

Report sheets

Every student gets a formatted report sheet carrying your school's name, address, and logo, with the comments already in place.

Term & annual results

Scores are saved per session, term and class. An annual card shows First, Second, Third and Cumulative results for every subject.

Cumulative reports

Generate a cumulative report sheet for one student or for the whole class, in the same format as your term reports.

Printing

Generate one report at a time or the whole class in one go, even 200 or more students, then print directly or save as PDF.

Online results

Publish a student's result to a secure online checker with a PIN, so parents can check it without a trip to the school.

Plans

Plans scale from a single classroom to a full school, from Basic up to Unlimited. Regenerating a report with identical scores never uses up your allowance.

SCHOOL MANAGEMENT & REPORT SHEET GENERATOR

Manage your school & Generate student report sheets from an Excel template with one click

🏫 School Information

Enter your school information and upload the school logo. This information will appear on every generated student report.

Step 0: Manage Classes

Add every class/arm your school uses (e.g. "SS2 Science 1"). Add each one once here — everyone then picks it from a dropdown everywhere else, so typos and inconsistent spelling/capitalization can't split one class's data into two.

Staff access

Give this school code to your teachers. On the sign-in page they tap “I'm a staff member joining a school”, create an account with the code, and you approve them here. Then assign each person a class (form master) or a subject in a class (subject teacher).

Step 1A: Master Student Class List

Choose the Academic Session, Term and Class. This becomes the context for everything below. Download the Class List Template, enter each student's name (Admission No is optional — use it only to tell apart two students with the same name), then upload it. The names are filled automatically into every subject sheet in Step 1B and into the General Master Template in Step 1C.

Don't see your class? Add it in Step 0 above first.

📷 Student Passport Photos

Add an optional passport photograph for each student. Photos are stored only in this browser/device and are not uploaded to Supabase. Use a clear JPG, PNG or WebP image.

Step 1B: Student Information & Subject Templates

The downloaded subject template already contains the Student Name and Admission No. from the class list above. The subject teacher only enters 1st CA, 2nd CA and Exams, then uploads the file here. Scores are saved separately for each Session and Term.

Step 1C: Download Master Template

Download the General Master Excel Template. It contains the class list and every subject score already uploaded by subject teachers for the selected Session and Term. Fill in anything missing, then upload the completed file in Step 2.

📊 Annual / Cumulative Performance

Combines the First, Second and Third Term scores saved for the selected Class and Session. Cumulative is the average of the terms that have scores. Click Show Cumulative Performance, pick a student from the list to generate one report, or generate every student's report at once. Each new cumulative report uses one report from your allowance, like a term report; regenerating the same report is free.

Step 2: Upload Completed Master Template

After completing the General Master Excel Template, upload it here to load the final class results into the report generator.
